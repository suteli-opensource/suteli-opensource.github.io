Verona Food Pantry (2022-2025)

When I was a Cub Scout, I got my first exposure to the food pantry while doing a food drive. I was always looking forward to help, as the workers there were always kind. When I became older, I decided to volunteer there myself, and enjoyed the time I spent helping others. I am glad I was able to help my local community, and work with some outstanding volunteers.
